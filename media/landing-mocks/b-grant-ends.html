Undying LedgerGitHub

An NGO pays for the app. Then the grant ends.

Savings groups keep their book in a tin box and, lately, an app. The app has a monthly bill.

The bill arrives. The book goes quiet.

After donor money ended in Kenya, 15 in 100 groups were still recording three months later.

On Midnight, nobody can switch it off.

When the vendor stops checking in, any member calls sunset. The vendor’s lock comes off and the group keeps recording. Fees come from a shared group key, so members pay nothing.

And her passbook opens only on her phone.

Amounts never go on chain. She can prove “at least 30 shares” to a lender without showing the rest.
